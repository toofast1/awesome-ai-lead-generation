## Social Listening & Intent Analysis
*Tools that monitor the web (Reddit, Twitter/X, LinkedIn) to find people actively looking for your solution.*

**Editor's choice** 🔥
- [Leado](https://leado.co) - AI-powered Reddit lead generation tool that monitors subreddits for high-intent leads and writes personalized replies automatically.
  
---

- [Awario](https://awario.com) - Enterprise-level brand monitoring and social listening tool.
- [Buska](https://buska.io) - Mentions monitoring with sentiment analysis.
- [F5Bot](https://f5bot.com) - Simple, free keyword alerts for Reddit.
- [GummySearch](https://gummysearch.com) - Audience research tool for Reddit to find communities and conversations.
- [Syften](https://syften.com) - Monitors multiple communities (Slack, Discourse, Reddit) for keywords.
- [Xquik](https://xquik.com/en) - X automation platform with REST API, MCP server, webhooks, and public X data tools.

## AI Search & GEO (AEO)
*Tools for Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) that monitor citations, benchmark brand visibility, and capture inbound leads from AI search engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini).*

**Editor's choice** 🔥
- [Picked](https://picked.so) - GEO platform that tracks AI search visibility and generates optimized content designed to rank on target keywords and get cited across Google, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Claude.

---

- [Otterly](https://otterly.ai) - AI search engine monitoring tool for tracking brand visibility and citations on ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews.
- [Peec AI](https://peec.ai) - Generative engine optimization tool measuring brand visibility, sentiment, and share of voice in LLM responses.
- [Profound](https://www.tryprofound.com/) - Enterprise AI search optimization platform that tracks brand visibility, prompt volumes, and citations across answer engines.

## Website Visitor De-anonymization
*Tools that identify the anonymous companies and people browsing your site to capture high-intent leads.*

- [Leadfeeder](https://www.dealfront.com/leadfeeder) - Identifies anonymous company traffic and scores leads based on on-site engagement.
- [RB2B](https://www.rb2b.com) - De-anonymizes website visitors into verified LinkedIn profiles and pushes them to Slack/CRM in real-time.
- [Snitcher](https://www.snitcher.com) - B2B website tracking that identifies the exact companies browsing your site and what pages they read.
- [Warmly](https://warmly.ai) - Autonomous website visitor identification, live chat, and automated outreach pipeline.

## Data Scraping & Enrichment
*Tools to turn a company name or profile into a verified email and data set.*

- [Apify](https://apify.com) - Extensive marketplace of pre-built scrapers (Google Maps, Instagram, TikTok, etc.).
- [Apollo](https://apollo.io) - Massive database of B2B contacts with built-in enrichment features.
- [Bright Data](https://brightdata.com) - Heavy-duty web data platform for large-scale extraction.
- [Browse AI](https://browse.ai) - No-code web automation to extract data from any website and monitor changes.
- [Clay](https://clay.com) - The spreadsheet that fills itself. Integrates 50+ data providers to enrich lead lists using AI.
- [Firecrawl](https://www.firecrawl.dev) - Web search and scraping API for AI agents, with company and people data through Firecrawl Alexandria.
- [Geonode](https://geonode.com) - Residential and datacenter proxy network with REST API access for web scraping pipelines.
- [PhantomBuster](https://phantombuster.com) - The standard for automating actions and scraping data from LinkedIn, Instagram, and Google Maps.
- [Vibe Prospecting](https://www.vibeprospecting.ai/) - AI-powered B2B prospecting platform for finding and enriching companies and decision-makers using natural-language prompts.

*Autonomous sales agents that research accounts, write hyper-personalized pitches, and handle objections to book meetings.*

- [AiSDR](https://aisdr.com) - AI sales agent that scores leads and automates outbound conversations until a meeting is booked.
- [Artisan](https://artisan.co) - AI B2B SDR platform (Ava) that consolidates lead data, personalized email writing, and automated booking.
- [Regie.ai](https://www.regie.ai) - Autonomous prospecting agents that generate outbound sequences tailored to buyer personas.
